# Body Clues

A tap-first log for food, drinks, symptoms, bathroom trips, sleep and pain, built to help spot patterns on an elimination diet.

- Everything is saved in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Use **Settings (gear) > Your data > Back up everything** to save a copy, and **Restore from backup** to bring it back on another device.
- To use it like an app on iPhone or iPad: open the page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

This is a personal log, not medical advice.
